PRIVACY

Short version: this page collects nothing. There is no account, no tracking, no cookies, no analytics.

  • The notify form stores your email only in your own browser (localStorage). It never leaves your device.
  • The search demo runs on rows built into this page. No queries are sent anywhere.
  • The drum pads synthesize sound with WebAudio on your device. No audio is uploaded or recorded.

When the real sample search launches, sources like Pixabay, Freesound, and the Internet Archive have their own terms and privacy policies. Their licenses govern the samples you use.
