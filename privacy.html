Privacy

This site collects nothing. No accounts, no login, no tracking, no analytics, no cookies. There is nothing to opt out of because there is nothing being collected.

The Sampler app itself searches third-party sample sources (Pixabay Sound Effects, Freesound, the Internet Archive). Any API keys you enter live in your own browser or machine. They are never sent to DOGS.

If you open an issue on the GitHub repo, that happens on GitHub, under GitHub's privacy policy.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/YEAHDOGS/sampler/issues.
